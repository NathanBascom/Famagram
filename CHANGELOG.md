# Changelog

New features in each Famagram release.

## [1.5.2] - 2026-10-13

### Added
- Stories: share a photo or short (up to 10 seconds) video that disappears
  after 48 hours. Add one by taking a photo/video with your camera or
  picking one from your library, from the ring at the top of your feed.
- Like stories, and — for your own — see who's viewed and liked them.
- A notification when someone you follow posts a new story.
- Save posts for later: a bookmark button on every post, and a new Saved
  page (bookmark icon in the header, next to notifications).

### Changed
- The delete-comment button is now black instead of light gray/red.
- Likes never trigger a notification — only comments and new stories do.
- Deleting a comment now asks for confirmation first, same as a post or
  story.
- Stories bar: a dedicated New Story button now sits at the far left,
  separated by a divider from the row of posted stories.

## [1.5.1] - 2026-10-12

### Added
- A Notifications tab (bell icon in the header) showing who commented on
  your posts, with an unread badge. Tapping one jumps straight to that post
  and comment thread.

### Changed
- Comment deletion: the author of a post can now delete any comment on it,
  in addition to the comment's own author and admins.
- Vids: the like/comment/share/mute buttons now line up with the username
  and caption instead of sitting noticeably higher.

## [1.5.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added
- See who liked a post — tap the like count to view the list of likers, not
  just the post's author, on both the feed and the Vids page.
- A Share button on the Vids page and on regular posts, using your device's
  native share sheet.
- Logo font customization (Admin > Settings > Logo Font): choose from 9
  typefaces for the app name in the header and login screen.
- 9 more Title Color options, for 19 total plus a custom color picker.

### Performance
- Videos load noticeably faster, both to start playing and when swiping to
  the next one on Vids.
- Videos now get a real thumbnail image, so you no longer see a black box
  before one loads, in the feed, a profile, or Vids.
- Revisiting a post you've already seen no longer re-downloads its photos
  or videos.

### Changed
- The profile page's "Follow" button is now labeled "Notifications" — it has
  always controlled whether you get notified about that person's new posts,
  not a social-style follow, so the label now matches what it does.
- Selecting a video for a post now limits you to one file at a time, matching
  the one-video-per-post rule, instead of offering a multi-photo-style picker.
- Unmuting (or muting) a video on the Vids page now carries over to the next
  video instead of resetting to muted every time.
- Profile pictures no longer trigger your phone's touch-and-hold zoom/save
  preview — that's reserved for actual post photos and videos.
- Find People's "Recent Searches" now remembers the people you tapped into,
  shown with their avatar and name, instead of the text you typed — tapping
  one goes straight to their profile.
- Admins can now only remove other members' posts, not edit their captions.

## [1.4.9] - 2026-10-11

### Added
- Find People: search for other family members by name or username, with
  your last 5 searches remembered for quick re-runs.
- Set your own username from the profile menu — shown on your profile and in
  search results.
- Multi-photo posts: add up to 10 photos per post with a swipeable carousel.
- Push notifications for comments: post authors can opt in to be notified
  when someone comments on their post, separate from the existing per-person
  "notify me when they post" option.
- Real pinch-to-zoom-and-pan on photos.
- Your app theme and background now follow you across devices instead of
  resetting per browser.
- Photos are automatically stripped of location and other metadata on
  upload, and iPhone photos are converted so they display correctly in every
  browser, not just Safari.
- Edit a post's caption after posting.
- "Sign out other devices" — end every other session on your account
  remotely, from the profile menu.
- A wider choice of dark background themes.
- Install Famagram to your home screen with offline support (PWA).
